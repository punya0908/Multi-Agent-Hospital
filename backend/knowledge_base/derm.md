# Dermatology Department (DERM)

department_id: DERM

## Overview and Location
The Dermatology department evaluates skin rashes, itching, acne, eczema, moles, hives, and hair or nail concerns.
Location: Block D, 1st Floor (Dermatology Suite D1).

## Pre-Visit Instructions
- Avoid applying makeup, foundation, heavy moisturizers, or medicated creams on the affected skin area on the day of your visit so the skin can be examined clearly.
- Wear clothing that makes the affected skin area easy to uncover during examination.
- Arrive 15 minutes prior to your appointment time.

## Registration Steps
- Check in at Block D, 1st Floor Dermatology Suite D1.
- Verify your registration details with the front desk coordinator.
- Wait in Dermatology Lounge D1 until your name is announced.

## What to Bring
- A written list or photos of all current skin products, soaps, sunscreens, and topical creams you use.
- Any prior allergy test results or dermatology prescription history.
- Valid photo ID and hospital registration details.
