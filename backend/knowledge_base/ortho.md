# Orthopaedics Department (ORTHO)

department_id: ORTHO

## Overview and Location
The Orthopaedics department provides specialist evaluation for bone, joint, muscle, back, knee, shoulder, and mobility-related concerns.
Location: Block B, Ground Floor (Orthopaedic Wing Reception).

## Pre-Visit Instructions
- Wear loose, comfortable clothing that allows easy access to the affected joint or limb (for example, shorts or wide-leg trousers for knee or ankle visits).
- Avoid applying heavy topical balms or bandages right before your visit unless instructed by a clinician.
- Arrive 20 minutes early if mobility assistance or a wheelchair is needed at the entrance.

## Registration Steps
- Check in at Block B, Ground Floor Orthopaedic Reception.
- Request mobility assistance at the front desk immediately if walking or standing is uncomfortable.
- Complete the joint and mobility intake questionnaire while waiting in Lounge B0.

## What to Bring
- Prior X-rays, MRI/CT scans, or radiology reports if available (digital copies or printed films).
- Valid photo ID and insurance or hospital registration card.
- List of current pain relievers or anti-inflammatory medications you have taken recently.
