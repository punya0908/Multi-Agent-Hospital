# Ear, Nose & Throat Department (ENT)

department_id: ENT

## Overview and Location
The Ear, Nose & Throat (ENT) department evaluates ear aches, ringing, hearing changes, sinus congestion, nasal issues, and throat discomfort.
Location: Block C, 2nd Floor (ENT Clinic Desk C2).

## Pre-Visit Instructions
- Avoid using over-the-counter ear drops or inserting cotton swabs for 24 hours prior to your appointment, if applicable, so the clinician can inspect the ear canal clearly.
- Avoid strong nasal sprays immediately before the visit unless prescribed for regular daily use.
- Arrive 15 minutes before your scheduled slot for registration.

## Registration Steps
- Report to Block C, 2nd Floor ENT Clinic Desk C2.
- Check in with your appointment confirmation and photo ID.
- Take a seat in ENT Waiting Area C2 until called by the nursing staff.

## What to Bring
- Prior audiometry (hearing test) reports or sinus scan records, if available.
- Valid government photo ID.
- List of current ear drops, nasal sprays, allergy tablets, or other medications.
