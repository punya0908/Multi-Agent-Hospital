# General Medicine Department (GEN_MED)

department_id: GEN_MED

## Overview and Location
The General Medicine department handles primary adult health evaluations, fever, fatigue, cough, headaches, and routine medical consultations.
Location: Block A, 1st Floor (Check-in Counter A1).

## Pre-Visit Instructions
- Arrive at least 15 minutes before your scheduled appointment time.
- Drink water normally unless your provider has previously instructed you to fast for blood work.
- Note the timeline and severity of your general symptoms to share at check-in.

## Registration Steps
- Present your photo ID at Block A, 1st Floor Registration Desk.
- Confirm your contact details and complete the standard intake form.
- Wait in Waiting Lounge A1 until your token number is called for vitals screening.

## What to Bring
- Valid government-issued photo ID.
- List of all current medications, vitamins, or supplements.
- Any recent thermometer readings, lab reports, or prior clinic notes.
