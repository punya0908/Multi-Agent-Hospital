# Ophthalmology Department (OPHTH)

department_id: OPHTH

## Overview and Location
The Ophthalmology department provides eye care for vision changes, eye redness, irritation, dryness, eye strain, and routine vision evaluations.
Location: Block C, 1st Floor (Eye Care Center C1).

## Pre-Visit Instructions
- Note that dilating eye drops may be used during your exam, which can cause temporary blurry near vision and light sensitivity for a few hours.
- Arrange for someone to accompany or drive you home if your pupils are dilated.
- Remove contact lenses before your exam if advised, and bring a lens case.

## Registration Steps
- Register at Block C, 1st Floor Eye Care Center C1 at least 20 minutes before your slot.
- Complete the preliminary vision screening form at check-in.
- Wait in Eye Care Lounge C1 for initial visual acuity testing.

## What to Bring
- Your current eyeglasses and/or contact lens prescription (along with your contact lens box or case).
- Sunglasses to wear after the visit in case your pupils are dilated.
- Valid photo ID and list of any eye drops or systemic medications you use.
