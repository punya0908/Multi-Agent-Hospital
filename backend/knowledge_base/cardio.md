# Cardiology Department (CARDIO)

department_id: CARDIO

## Overview and Location
The Cardiology department handles non-emergency outpatient consultations for blood pressure follow-ups, cholesterol management, palpitations review, and routine cardiac checkups.
Location: Block A, 3rd Floor (Cardiac Outpatient Wing A3).

## Pre-Visit Instructions
- Take your regular prescribed medications on schedule unless a clinician specifically told you otherwise.
- Avoid heavy caffeine or strenuous exercise for at least 2 hours before a scheduled blood pressure or ECG evaluation.
- Arrive 20 minutes early to allow time for resting vitals measurement.

## Registration Steps
- Check in at Block A, 3rd Floor Cardiac Outpatient Wing A3.
- Complete the cardiovascular history checklist at the desk.
- Rest quietly in Waiting Area A3 for 5–10 minutes prior to blood pressure measurement.

## What to Bring
- Prior ECG reports, echocardiogram summaries, and home blood pressure logs.
- A complete, up-to-date list of all current medications and dosages.
- Valid photo ID and recent lipid profile or blood test reports.
